# PromiseGraph

### Your commitments, connected.

PromiseGraph is an AI-powered communication intelligence platform that transforms conversations into trackable commitments.

Important promises often get lost across emails, WhatsApp messages, and team conversations. PromiseGraph aims to connect these conversations, identify commitments, track deadlines, and discover evidence of completed work.

## Core Features

- **Commitment Extraction:** Identify promises, tasks, owners, and deadlines from conversations.
- **People Graph:** Connect people and their commitments across communication platforms.
- **Commitment Tracking:** Monitor pending, completed, and overdue commitments.
- **Evidence Verification:** Find related evidence of completed work.
- **Smart Follow-ups:** Prepare context-aware follow-up messages for user approval.

## Planned Integrations
